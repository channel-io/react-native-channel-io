# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in react-native-channel-plugin.

This SDK is distributed under the [Channel io SDK LICENSE](LICENSE) and is developed by the Channel Team. **We do not accept external pull requests**, and pull requests from outside the team will be closed without being merged.

We still welcome your feedback.

- **Bug reports and feature requests**: please open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/channel-io/react-native-channel-io/issues). Including your `react-native-channel-plugin` version, React Native version, platform, and reproduction steps helps us a lot.
- **Questions and support**: please contact us via Channel Talk for a faster response.

If you have found a fix, feel free to describe it in an issue. Our team will review it and, if appropriate, apply the change in an upcoming release.
